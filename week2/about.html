About the Training & Placement Department

Anil Neerukonda Institute of Technology & Sciences (ANITS) has a dedicated Training & Placement Department...

ANITS provides comprehensive Campus Recruitment Training (CRT) covering Quantitative Aptitude, Logical Reasoning, Verbal Reasoning, English Communication, Group Discussions and Interview Skills.

Through dedicated efforts and industry collaboration, ANITS has consistently achieved excellent placement records.

Our Department Focuses On

  • Identifying employment opportunities.
  • Building strong industry partnerships.
  • Providing campus recruitment training.
  • Organizing workshops and expert lectures.
  • Promoting innovation and entrepreneurship.
  • Facilitating internships and industrial visits.
